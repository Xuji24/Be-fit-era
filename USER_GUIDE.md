# Welcome to Be Fit Era! 🏃‍♂️💪

Welcome to the **Be Fit Era** community! We're thrilled to have you on board. Here is a quick guide to help you get started on your fitness journey right after signing up and logging in.

## 1. Complete Your Profile 👤
Head over to the **Profile** page (`/profile`):
- Add a profile picture so the community can recognize you.
- Update your preferred settings (notification preferences, display name, etc.).

## 2. Connect Your Fitness Trackers 🔗
To make logging your progress seamless:
- Go to the **Settings** or **Profile** page.
- Look for the **Connect to Strava** option. Connecting Strava automatically imports your runs, rides, and workouts directly into Be Fit Era, counting towards your active race challenges.

## 3. Explore & Join a Race 🏁
Ready for an active challenge? 
- Navigate to the **Races** page (`/race`).
- Browse through the upcoming 5K, 10K, Half Marathons, or other custom challenges.
- Click on a race to read the details, review the target distance, and secure your spot by completing the registration form.

## 4. Log Your Activities 🏃‍♀️
If you prefer not to use an automated tracker like Strava, or if you did an offline workout:
- Go to the **Activities** page (`/activities`).
- Manually log your activity (distance, time, date). Every kilometer counts toward your race goals and leaderboard standing!

## 5. Check the Leaderboard 🏆
See how you stack up against the rest of the community:
- Visit the **Leaderboard** (`/leaderboard`) to check rankings.
- Stay motivated by tracking the top runners and challenging yourself to climb the ranks!

## 6. Become an Organizer (Optional) 🌟
Are you part of a fitness group or planning your own event? 
- Head to the **Organizer** page.
- Submit an Organizer Application with your details and proof of authorization.
- Once approved by an Admin, you can start creating and managing your own spectacular races on the platform!

Enjoy your time at Be Fit Era and let's crush those fitness goals! 🎉
